# Changelog
## 1.0
- Lapis app-window display backend, 1-bit renderer with auto-exposure and Atkinson dithering, light boost.
- Built-in USB Xbox 360 / One / Series (incl. Elite Series 2) driver over usbfs, with GIP init and port reset.
- Sound effects and OPL music through PipeWire/ALSA.
- Lapis sensor controls, quit combos, "DOOM (launcher)" dock, Bring Your Own WAD with optional
  checksum-verified shareware download.
